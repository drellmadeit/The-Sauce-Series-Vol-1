The Sauce Suite __VERSION__

An all-in-one vocal plugin for rap, melodic rap, trap-soul and R&B, by Drellmadeit.

  • Sauce Assistant: listens to your beat and sets up a vocal chain that sits in the mix
  • 943 presets: every genre plus 152 artist styles with lead, hook, ad-lib and background versions
  • Fully rearrangeable chain with De-Esser, De-Pop, 12-band dynamic EQ and resonance suppressor
  • Auto Tune with Auto Key: it finds your song's key for you
  • Mix / Tracking latency: down to 0.5 ms for recording
  • Vocal Coach, Match EQ, ducking delay and reverb, limiter

This installs the Audio Unit (Logic Pro, GarageBand) and VST3 (FL Studio, Ableton Live, Studio One, Cubase, Reaper) versions for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.
