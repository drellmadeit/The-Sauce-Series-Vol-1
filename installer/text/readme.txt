THE SAUCE SUITE - QUICK START

1. Open your DAW and rescan plugins.
   FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins > Find more plugins.
2. Add "The Sauce Suite" (by Drellmadeit) to your lead vocal track.
3. Quickest start: click SAUCE ASSISTANT, pick your genre / artist sound and the part
   (Lead, Hook, Ad-libs, Backgrounds...), route your beat in, press Start listening,
   play the song, then "Make it sit in the mix". Use the feedback buttons to fine-tune.
   Or pick a GENRE (loads its Lead preset), an ARTIST (only artists in that genre show),
   or any PRESET yourself. Drag the module tabs to change the vocal chain order:
   the sound runs through the tabs from left to right.
4. No sidechain? Put The Sauce Suite on your beat track, click LINK there and choose
   "Send this track as the beat". On your vocal, click LINK and pick that track.
   Or just drag your beat file onto the Sauce Assistant.
4. Auto Tune tab: click "Auto Key" and sing (or route your beat in) and it sets the key.
5. Recording? Click "Tracking" in the METERS panel for ~0.5 ms latency.
   Mixing? Use "Mix" for the best quality.
6. Save your own sound with "Save As...". Factory presets are never overwritten.

Route your beat to the plugin's sidechain for the white spectrum and Vocal Coach
clash checks: click "How to route" in the PRESET & ROUTING panel.

Uninstall: Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > The Sauce Suite > Uninstall.
