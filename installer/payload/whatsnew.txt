THE SAUCE SUITE - WHAT'S NEW

1.4.0 - 2026-11-24
- Sauce Assistant (new button at the top of the plugin). Tell it the sound you want (a genre, an artist sound, or "Match the beat"), and the part: Lead, Hook, Ad-libs, Backgrounds, Harmonies or Doubles. Then play your beat through the sidechain for 10-20 seconds. It:
  - measures the beat (how much 808/sub, crowded low mids, where the vocal has room, how bright the hats are, how dense it is), takes the tempo from your DAW and detects the key;
  - guesses the genre when you choose "Match the beat" and shows how sure it is;
  - starts from the best matching preset, then tailors it to this beat: high-pass vs the 808, a cut where the beat is crowded (180-600 Hz), a lift in the beat's open spot (1.5-5 kHz), air/de-ess vs the hats, compression vs density, reverb/delay ducking vs busyness, tempo-synced delay, reverb pre-delay and tail, and Auto Tune key;
  - sets the vocal level against the beat from your actual processed vocal (lead louder, ad-libs and backgrounds tucked under);
  - explains every change in plain words, has Before/After, and takes feedback: More polished, More raw, Wetter, Drier, Brighter, Darker, Vocal louder/quieter, Harder/Softer tune.
- Genre and Artist menus now change the sound. Picking a genre loads its Lead preset; picking an artist loads that artist's preset. A pop-up confirms what changed (chain, tune, compression, reverb, delay, grit).
- Artists are listed only under the genres that fit them: Drake, J. Cole, Rylo Rodriguez and others under Rap/Trap; Bryson Tiller, Tory Lanez, Chris Brown, SZA, Summer Walker and others under R&B; every music genre has its own artists (89 artist-inspired starting points, up from 11, all unofficial).
- Rearrangeable vocal chain. Drag the module tabs (Gate, Auto Tune, EQ, Match EQ, Vocal FX, Compressor) into any order; the arrows show the signal flow and the Limiter always stays last. The order is saved with presets and DAW sessions, and presets set their own order (e.g. Lo-Fi and Vintage saturate first; Boom Bap, Drill, Rock and Aggressive compress before EQ). Reordering never changes latency. Gear menu > "Reset vocal chain order".
- 421 factory presets (up from 240). Each music genre now has 18 roles, including Hook (Stadium), Ad-libs (Phone Filter), Ad-libs (Echo Throw), Backgrounds (Airy Pad), Harmonies (Wide) and Intro / Whisper.
- 96 automated checks (was 62), all passing on Linux and the Windows build.

1.3.1 - 2026-11-24
- Fix: "The procedure entry point TaskDialogIndirect could not be located ... ilbridge.exe". The Windows plugin now embeds the Common Controls 6 manifest (resource type 24, ID 2), as Microsoft's compiler does for JUCE plugins. FL Studio's plugin bridge loads it correctly.
- The plugin's Windows file version now shows the real version (it showed 1.0.0 before).
- Build-Windows.bat: one-click native build with Microsoft Visual C++ on your own PC. It runs every test there and installs the VST3. JUCE is downloaded as a zip, so Git isn't needed.

1.3.0 - 2026-11-24
- Latency switch (Meters panel): Mix | Tracking.
  - Mix: best quality, about 16 ms (762 samples at 48 kHz), fully compensated by the DAW. Use it for mixing.
  - Tracking: about 0.5 ms reported (24 samples). Breaths, consonants and every module except Auto Tune arrive in 0.5 ms; tuned notes arrive one cycle of the sung note later (2.8 ms at 440 Hz, 5.0 ms at 220 Hz, 7.3 ms at 147 Hz). The limiter has no look-ahead in this mode and still holds the ceiling exactly.
- Auto Key (Auto Tune page). Detects the song's key from your vocal's pitch, plus the beat's chords when routed to the sidechain, and sets Key/Scale. Follows key changes (about 20 s memory). Turn it off to lock the key. It runs in the background analysis, so it adds 0 ms latency.
- Presets never change the latency mode or Auto Key.
- 60 automated checks (was 47).
